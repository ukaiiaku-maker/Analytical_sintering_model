# Next handoff implementation decision

This recommendation is conditional and not validation. Do not tune intrinsic GB mobility: its bounded envelope already failed to rescue the pathway. Preserve the nucleation barrier and diffusivities.

Open-channel recovery is required because it restores most of the fast density deficit, but it is not sufficient for a Chen window. Multiplying closed rate alone cannot help the fast ramp without usable closed inventory, while multiplying inventory diverts open pore volume and can reduce density. The availability-balanced law behaves conservatively but exposes that closed availability is too small.

The next implementation should address a jointly measurable preparation-and-rate law: how PR-prepared inventory persists as shrinkable closed porosity, its calibrated removal time, and how open shrinkage remains active until that closed capacity is demonstrably available. No destructive ablation is interpretable until such a parent law produces a finite strict window.
