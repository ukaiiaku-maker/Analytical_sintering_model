# ZrO2 forward Chen failure decomposition

Using the actual T1→T2 transition state, the 84 baseline points classify as 8 low-temperature nucleation/sink limited, 8 low-temperature excess-power/connected-pore-redistribution limited, 56 first-step overgrown, and 12 high-temperature pore-coarsening/pinning-loss failures.

The density-exhaustion boundary is at approximately 1150 °C, while excessive growth begins between 1050 and 1150 °C depending on prepared state. Thus the lower density boundary is at or above the upper growth boundary: this is primarily a boundary-ordering failure. At 1050 °C activity is too weak; at 1150 °C excess surface power and slow removal dominate; at 1250–1300 °C pore coarsening removes pinning and growth dominates. Closed fractions and closed-shrinkage rates remain too small to control the boundary.

No finite Chen band exists. The exact experimental TSS schedule is unavailable, and closed accommodation is only a bounded proxy.
