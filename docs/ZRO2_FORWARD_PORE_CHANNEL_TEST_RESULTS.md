# Pore-channel baseline test results

## Analytical behavior

All limiting tests pass. Surface coarsening and PR pinch-off have zero direct density
rate; regularization/damage transfers conserve pore volume; stores remain
nonnegative; the density identity holds; closed shrinkage stops with zero inventory
or nonpositive stress; gas reduces driving stress; surface time increases with
(r^4); and smaller pores exert stronger Zener pressure at fixed pore fraction.

The PR scan shows the expected transition from stable/weakly pinching short segments
to high pinch probability at large \(\lambda_{seg}/r\). Increasing
\(\gamma_{GB}/\gamma_s\) shifts the bounded geometry factor. Moderate activity and
narrow distributions favor regularization; low activity and broad tails favor
damage. These are qualitative response tests, not calibrated transition boundaries.

## Heating-rate and two-step outcomes

None of the 0.2–100 °C/min families jointly attains the requested density targets
from 0.75 through 0.95 for a matched-density comparison. Maximum final density is
about 0.77 for coarsening-only and about 0.72 for the pinch-off modes. Therefore
the requested density spans above grain-size ratios 1.2, 1.5, and 2.0 are zero/not
resolved, and no extrapolated matched-density ratio is reported. Grain growth becomes
large before sufficient densification.

Across four fixed first-step states and all three topology modes, there are zero
strict successes and zero finite Chen windows. The naturally prepared state contains
only density-exhaustion and mixed/growth failures. The process-map gate fails, so
failure-mode panels replace success-colored maps.

## Ablations and ledger

Removing closed shrinkage eliminates late density gain. Disabling Zener pinning
increases growth. High-level topology removal changes the balance of open and closed
shrinkage, but no ablation produces a promotable window. The reconstructed selected-
path ledger still has budget violations; topology power is recorded as named
diagnostic channels and no rate is silently rescaled.

The dominant defect is insufficient usable closed inventory/accommodation together
with aggressive grain growth and unresolved ledger consistency. PR regularization,
damage, and pinch-off alter state but do not create the required handoff. All barrier
calls are below the fitted range and extrapolative.

This is not validation. No validation claim is made.
