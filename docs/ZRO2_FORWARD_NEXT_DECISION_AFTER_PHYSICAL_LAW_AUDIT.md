is dominated by the product of closed inventory and accommodation, not by a missing
abstract activation energy. Increasing a generic prefactor or fitting `Q_closed`
would conceal that state-mapping problem.

   specify specimen geometry for external surface area.

4. Constrain trapped gas from atmosphere, closure temperature, and pore-volume
   history rather than a pressure fraction.
5. Audit a local capillary-stress trajectory against the full energy ledger without
   feeding grain-growth power back into activation stress.
6. Obtain barrier data in the actual 850–1500 °C domain or explicitly quantify the
   uncertainty caused by the 1557 °C endpoint clamp.
