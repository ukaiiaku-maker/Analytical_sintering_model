# Confirmed Separated-Mechanism Synthesis

No common production candidate is confirmed. Fast firing is nucleation-limitation-sensitive, but zero-PR does not remove it. Thus the current hits cannot support the proposed competition between nucleation waiting and PR/surface damage. Their ratios are moderate (about 2.1--2.8 for the seed ablations), not numerically extreme, but the causal pathway is wrong.

Because no material passed, topology closures were not searched dynamically and no Chen window was promoted.
