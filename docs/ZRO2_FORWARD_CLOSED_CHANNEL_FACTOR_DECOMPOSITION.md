# Closed-channel factor decomposition

This decomposition is diagnostic and not validation. Open-only representative states are limited by no closed inventory. Supported closed states are controlled by coupled small factors: renewal activity is commonly very small, the dimensional rate scale is finite, and available inventory and size weighting vary strongly by state. Accommodation can be high while the rate remains small, so accommodation alone is not evidence of useful closed shrinkage.

Required-rate postprocessing shows the resolved low-T2 path needs about 285× its current integrated closed contribution, while its near-boundary and high-T2 paths need order-unity closed contributions but are already growth-limited. Candidate-state injection reduces the low-T2 requirement to about 26× yet still does not create a window. These multipliers are analytical diagnostics only and were not applied.

Hypothetical multiplier estimates also show a preservation tradeoff: sufficiently strong density recovery can erase the lower densification-exhaustion boundary. No multiplier is accepted as model physics.
