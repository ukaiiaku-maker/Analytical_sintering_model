# Candidate 693168: revised interpretation

Candidate 693168 remains a **conditional Tier B** exact dynamic qualitative mechanism candidate. Its high-temperature reference reaches roughly 858–1123 nm over density 0.95–0.98 while the audited two-step path remains roughly 118 nm. The median reduction is about 86–89%, depending on timestep. Both paths attain the interval, density interpolation is supported, and the result survives 30-, 15-, and 5-minute timestep limits. This is a plausible experimental-scale two-step response, not a defect caused by ratio magnitude.

The complete window is approximately 260–280 °C wide depending on timestep. Density exhaustion bounds it below because closed-pore shrinkage/accommodation is too slow within the common budget; thermally activated grain growth bounds it above. PR damage, closed transition, closed shrinkage, and finite accommodation destroy the joint result when ablated.

The candidate remains Tier B rather than Tier A for three reasons: first-step growth is about 13.7%, exceeding the 5% Tier-A criterion; the closed store already represents about 65% of remaining pore volume at the switch and approaches saturation near the target; and neither closed-pore accommodation nor high-temperature grain-growth kinetics has been quantitatively calibrated. The predicted closed fraction and accommodation trajectory—not the large grain-size separation—are the main falsification targets.

The model makes no validation or paper-readiness claim. Required measurements are matched-density absolute grain size, 3D connected and closed porosity, internal gas pressure or a defensible accommodation proxy, and interrupted-step pore distributions.
