# Grain-growth-driven pore coalescence memory

## Result

This broad mechanism search completed 1,000,000 vectorized conditions, 20,000 reduced dynamic candidates, 1,000 exact integrations, and 50 production-ranked cases. One exact trajectory near-hit attained 0.98 on both paths and produced a median two-step grain-size reduction of 22.1%, a maximum reduction of 27.4%, and a density span of 0.0223 above 20% reduction. It therefore met the trajectory portion of Tier B.

It is not an accepted Tier B candidate: no exact candidate produced a complete Chen window with both lower densification-exhaustion and upper grain-growth boundaries. The near-hit is retained as Tier C mechanism evidence. Fast-firing preservation was not promoted because the complete two-step acceptance gate failed.

## Mechanism

Boundary sweep is computed from the instantaneous fractional grain-growth rate. It transfers connected fine-pore volume conservatively into fewer large attached or TJ pores, can detach those pores into isolated volume, can recapture isolated volume, and transitions isolated volume into a closed store. Only named open- and closed-pore shrinkage fluxes change density. Attached large pores and persistent junction state affect migration only.

## Interpretation

Unlike the preceding latent-memory model, explicit coalescence can generate the requested high-density trajectory separation in mean grain size. The failure moved to map topology: closed-pore support and sweep memory eliminate or fail to bracket the lower boundary. The next bounded test should focus on exhaustion of closed-pore accommodation at low T2, not enlarge the coalescence search or alter nucleation-limited material barriers.

No validation or paper-readiness claim is made.
