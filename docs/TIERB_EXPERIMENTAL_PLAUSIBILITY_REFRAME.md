# Tier-B experimental-plausibility reframe

## Scientific correction

Large high-temperature versus two-step grain-size separation is not inherently implausible. Two-step sintering is interesting precisely because a high-temperature reference may coarsen into the submicron or micron range while a properly prepared two-step path retains nanoscale grains. Ratio magnitude is therefore not an artifact test. A large separation is rejected only when it depends on an unsupported density interval, timestep collapse, hidden density bookkeeping, negative or unbounded states, unexplained grain-size decrease, pore-store bypass, or failed causal ablations.

## Revised tiers

- **Tier A:** quantitatively calibrated or calibration-ready; exact dynamic confirmation; complete Chen window; attained high-density trajectory separation; growth tolerances satisfied; closed-pore/accommodation state experimentally constrained; fast-firing envelope preserved; and causal ablations complete.
- **Tier B:** exact dynamic qualitative mechanism candidate with a complete Chen window, attained high-density separation, and an experimentally plausible response magnitude. It is not quantitatively calibrated. Closed-pore/accommodation predictions remain falsification targets. Tier B supports mechanistic discussion and figure development, not validation.
- **Tier C:** partial mechanism evidence, including only one target behavior, relaxed growth tolerance, incomplete boundary bracketing, lower-density-only support, or noncausal ablation behavior.

## Six-candidate result

All six exact candidates remain Tier B. None is downgraded because of a large ratio. Candidate 693168 is calibration-sensitive because its first-step growth is about 13.7%, its closed-store fraction is about 65% at the switch, and closed accommodation is not experimentally constrained. Candidates with near-saturated closed stores or limited robustness coverage are also calibration-sensitive. Candidates 822940 and 366094 are useful lower-closed-fraction comparators.

## Plausibility screen

The screen records absolute grain sizes, joint density attainment, timestep evidence, closed-store saturation, causal ablations, and boundedness separately. Submicron/micron high-temperature grains, 50–300 nm two-step grains, and reductions above 50% are allowed. A high closed fraction triggers calibration priority, not automatic rejection. The current six-candidate records contain no explicit artifact under these rules.

## Mechanistic separation

Fast firing remains a nucleation-limited material-kinetic result: nucleation-facile ablation removes it, PR-off may preserve it, and transport-only or exchange-limited variants do not reproduce it. Two-step behavior remains a distinct PR-prepared closed-pore/accommodation mechanism: the lower boundary is insufficient closed shrinkage/accommodation and the upper boundary is thermally activated grain growth.

## Calibration and falsification

The priority experiment is an interrupted first/second-step series measuring absolute `G(rho)`, 3D open/connected/closed porosity, pore-number and size distributions, and an internal gas-pressure or accommodation proxy. These data would test whether the predicted switch-state closed fraction, near-target closed population, and closed-shrinkage share are physically realized. The result remains uncalibrated, unvalidated, and not paper-ready.
