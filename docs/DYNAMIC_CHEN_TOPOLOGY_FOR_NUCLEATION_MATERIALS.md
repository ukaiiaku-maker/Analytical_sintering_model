# Dynamic Chen Topology for Nucleation Materials

The material kinetics of E0021 and E0142 were frozen. A 128-closure fractional topology search evaluated 256 material/topology combinations using exact first-step state transfer and fully evolving second steps. The coarse screen retained preparation growth below 20%, required practical T2<T1, and preserved lower density and upper growth failures.

The screen produced eight coarse finite-window combinations, 37 isolated near hits, and 211 rejections. Twelve promising closure/material rows were refined dynamically at 10 C resolution across four representative preparation states. Thirty-seven state-specific finite windows survived: eight for E0021 and 29 for E0142. The largest E0021 interval was 1190--1290 C; the largest E0142 interval was 1060--1240 C, both for G0=600 nm, T1=1500 C, and switch density 0.88.

These are bounded-map confirmations. The requested full G0/T1/heating-rate/switch grid, 5% preparation filters, and production triplet assemblies remain pending.
