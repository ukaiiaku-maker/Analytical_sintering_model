# ZrO2 950 °C sensitivity audit

The previous PDF-conditioned branch separated the green-state full-process prediction from the observed-interval comparison. This audit preserves that separation and treats 950 °C, density 0.66, and grain size 50 nm as a controlled common comparison state, never as a method-specific fitted state.

The primary factorial contains 405 common states, plus two explicitly tagged closed-initial-state diagnostics. Mean 50 °C/min final density changes by 0.178 across pore-D50 levels (0.974 at 10 nm to 0.796 at 60 nm), far more than the 0.018 rho-start effect, 0.026 pore-width effect, 0.028 isolation effect, or negligible initial-grain-size effect. Initial pore D50 is therefore the dominant mapped factor. The nominal fast smaller-grain sign remains, but it is not robust across the whole factorial; smaller-D90 behavior also remains weak and state-dependent.

Microwave LMS/HMS data remain comparison targets, not thermal validation. Barrier extrapolation below its fitted range remains a limitation, closed accommodation is a bounded proxy unless calibrated, and no validation claim is made.
