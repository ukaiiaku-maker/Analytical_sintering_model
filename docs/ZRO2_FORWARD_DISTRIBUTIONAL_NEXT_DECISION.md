# Distributional model next decision

## Decision

Final label: `diagnostic_negative_result`.

Distributional evolution passes conservation and limiting checks and generates some synthetic density/grain success points, but it does not generate a lower/success/upper topology. It is not promoted as a physical or semi-phenomenological candidate.

## Dominant failure

The primary defect remains first-step preparation of useful closed inventory coupled to a selective closed-shrinkage rate. The tested distribution dynamics do not solve the selectivity problem:

- high useful closed inventory yields success already at the lowest T2;
- lower inventory yields density exhaustion;
- regularization, damage, and distributional Zener are not causal in the best case;
- gas pressure can suppress shrinkage but does not create a finite intermediate band;
- accommodation is not the sole limiter because infinite accommodation preserves the missing-lower-boundary defect;
- the energy-ledger gate is not causal in the selected success and ledger consistency remains unresolved.
- the heating-rate response has the wrong sign in 387 of 399 attained comparisons, so the distribution state does not provide the required fast-ramp grain-growth suppression.

## Next evidence

Do not broaden the process search or tune material parameters. The next defensible step is to derive or measure a preparation-dependent shrinkability/accommodation distribution that changes with pore origin and age while retaining the fixed transport kernels, then require destructive PR/transition/Zener ablations to remove any recovered window.

True positive topology-labelled pore bins remain unavailable, so candidate 693168 remains a response-form target only. No physical `Q_closed` should be introduced. No validation claim is made.
