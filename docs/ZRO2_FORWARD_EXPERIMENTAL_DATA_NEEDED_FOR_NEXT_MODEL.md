# Experimental data needed before the next model

The highest-priority inputs are:

1. Common-state pore D50, D90, width, number distribution, and connectivity at 950 °C.
2. The exact Mazaheri TSS heating rates, T1, switch criterion/time, T2, and hold.
3. Interrupted density, grain size, open/isolated/closed pore fraction, and pore distribution at the first-step switch and during step 2.
4. Pore-boundary attachment and boundary-mobility evidence to separate Zener pinning from mobile drag.
5. Low-temperature stress-dependent barrier or creep data over 1000–1300 °C.
6. Closure density, trapped-gas state, and closed-pore shrinkage kinetics.

Without these data, changing closed shrinkage, pinning, or barrier extrapolation would be poorly identifiable and vulnerable to target fitting. Microwave HMS/LMS measurements require matched thermal controls before microwave-specific physics is introduced. The current model is not validated.
