# ZrO2 Chen failure mechanism audit

The conditioned 195-case map still has zero strict successes and zero finite windows. Of 60 first-step groups, 28 have both estimable boundaries; every estimated gap is negative, from -100 to -200 °C. Another 32 groups have a missing or censored boundary. The principal result is therefore both boundary ordering and boundary censoring: density requires a higher T2 than the strict grain limit permits.

Path-level classifications include 60 unattained first steps, 55 mixed density-and-growth failures, 68 high-temperature pin-loss/growth-activation failures, and 12 low-temperature sink-limited cases. These labels are diagnostic attribution from existing observables, not new constitutive mechanisms.

The 950 °C state remains common and controlled. Barrier extrapolation remains a limitation; closed accommodation remains a bounded proxy. Microwave measurements are not thermal validation, and no validation claim is made.
