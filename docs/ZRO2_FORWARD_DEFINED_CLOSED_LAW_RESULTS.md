# Defined closed-law results

The current resolved proxy reproduced its inherited PDF-conditioned ramp results: density 0.937011 and grain size 0.937857 µm at 5 °C/min; density 0.900504 and grain size 0.258471 µm at 50 °C/min. Other binwise laws changed little because these ramps naturally formed only about `2.05e-4` closed volume at 5 °C/min and `2.7e-5` at 50 °C/min.

In the injected candidate state, the reduced transfer produced one strict point while retaining low-density and high-growth failures. GB modes produced ten strict points but universally densified at low T2, losing the lower boundary. Gas accommodation also lost the lower boundary. Renewal and empirical mappings were too slow or growth-limited; surface accommodation produced no closed density removal.

Naturally prepared states produced zero strict successes in every mode. GB/gas mappings restored closed removal but not the required topology. Renewal preserved low-temperature exhaustion and approached the density target (`0.975621` maximum for `m=3`) without strict success. No mode passed the fixed-path gate, so the mini-map was not run: zero mini-map successes and zero finite windows.

Failure is primarily state preparation and unresolved parameter mapping, not failure to port the defined law structure. No validation is claimed.
