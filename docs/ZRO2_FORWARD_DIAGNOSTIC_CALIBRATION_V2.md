# ZrO2 forward diagnostic calibration v2

This campaign diagnoses commit `12ac45a`; it makes no validation claim. The baseline matches the CS endpoints (density 0.97538, grain size 2.1407 µm) but reaches density 0.65 about 330 °C later than the digitized curve.

Endpoint-only, density-curve-only, and density-plus-grain objectives were kept distinct. The best bounded curve calibration used a site-density multiplier of 160 and stress/work factor of 10, yet retained a 247 °C onset error, overshot final density to 0.9998, and did not improve on RMSE 0.103. These factors are already extreme relative to baseline 39.5 and 1. Full CS trajectory calibration is therefore not achieved by compact geometry scaling alone.

The exact barrier fit begins at 1557 °C; microwave HMS timing is outside a purely thermal validation; the TSS schedule remains unavailable; closed accommodation remains a bounded proxy.
