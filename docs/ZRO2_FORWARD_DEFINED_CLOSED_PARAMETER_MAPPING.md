# Defined closed-law parameter mapping

Fixed/transferred inputs are `Gstar(sigma,T)`, attempt frequency, Burgers-vector scale, `D_GB0=0.056 m²/s`, `Q_GB=380 kJ/mol`, `D_s0=0.10 m²/s`, `Q_s=380 kJ/mol`, surface/GB energies, and atomic volume. Triple-line density, event strain, closed stress, and Zener radius are geometry-derived.

Pore stores, closed count/radius, gas pressure, accommodation used/recovered, and PR memory/work are evolved states. Renewal times, activity, fluxes, apparent slopes, and migration rates are computed diagnostics.

The unresolved physical mappings are closed exchange time, transport length, shrinkable fraction, coordination/dihedral geometry, trapped-gas content, PR-to-closed transition rate, and accommodation capacity/consumption/recovery. The GB prefactor is semi-phenomenological unless dimensionally derived. Candidate parameters remain reduced phenomenological. `Q_closed_app` and empirical closure parameters remain diagnostic only.
