# Limitations

- The barrier fit begins at 1557 °C, above most target data; lower-temperature calculations use a flagged nearest-slice clamp.
- Figure curves are visually digitized from raster panels and carry stated uncertainty.
- Exact prior-publication TSS path unavailable.
- Absolute GB mobility prefactor and the global site-density multiplier are one-time CS calibrations, not independent measurements.
- CS endpoints match, but the modeled density onset is substantially later than the digitized CS trajectory.
- The thermal 50 °C/min calculation misses the microwave HMS density target substantially.
- The explored two-level map has no case meeting density and grain-size criteria simultaneously.
- Closed accommodation is explicitly a bounded proxy, not a calibrated gas-pressure model.
- ScSZ diffusivities are transferred as a zirconia baseline and not composition-specific 8YSZ measurements.
