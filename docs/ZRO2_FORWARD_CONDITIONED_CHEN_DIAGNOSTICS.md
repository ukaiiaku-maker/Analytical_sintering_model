# PDF-conditioned Chen diagnostics

The conditioned map starts from the common 950 °C state and covers physically ordered combinations of T1 1200–1500 °C, T2 1000–1300 °C, switch density 0.70–0.90, and holds of 5, 10, 20, 30, and 40 h. It contains 195 cases after excluding `T2 >= T1`.

There are zero strict density-plus-0.29 µm successes and no finite windows. There are also zero density-qualified cases below 0.5 µm; only eight are below 1.0 µm. Failures comprise 124 lower-density-exhaustion cases, 60 unattained first-step switches, and 11 upper-growth failures under the strict classification. The best target-distance case reaches density 0.9509 and grain size 0.595 µm.

Conditioning therefore does not create a TSS-like band. The failure remains boundary-ordering/target-stringency limited: lower-temperature paths lack density, while paths attaining density exceed the ultrafine grain target. The exact Mazaheri TSS schedule remains unavailable.
