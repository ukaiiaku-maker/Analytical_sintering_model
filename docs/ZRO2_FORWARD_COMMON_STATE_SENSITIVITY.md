# Common-state sensitivity

For every factorial condition, the 5 and 50 °C/min paths receive identical cloned pore arrays and the same recorded density, grain size, pore scale, width, isolated fraction, and closed fraction. The 405 primary states all have zero initial closed porosity; only two separately tagged diagnostics use nonzero closed fractions. CS, LMS, HMS, and TSS are not assigned separate initial states.

Pore D50 dominates 50 °C/min density attainment. Across the broad factorial, the fast smaller-grain sign occurs over most matched densities in only 32 of 405 primary conditions, while the smaller-D90 sign does so in 43. Thus the nominal fast-grain result survives locally but is sensitive to the common pore state, and the D90 sign remains weak.

The earlier onset discrepancy remains an initialization/pre-950 °C prediction mismatch plus possible barrier extrapolation, not solely a barrier-onset error. Microwave targets are comparisons, relaxed outcomes are diagnostic, and this is not validation.
