# Required Chen physics-gap analysis

This read-only analysis changes no model physics and reruns no simulations. The strict Mazaheri-style target (density 0.976, grain size 0.29 µm) still has zero successes and zero finite windows.

Among 60 first-step groups, 28 have both boundary estimates. Every one has a negative gap: minimum and median −200 °C, maximum −100 °C. Creating a sampled 25 °C-wide window therefore requires at least 125 °C of favorable combined boundary motion. Eighteen groups lack a density boundary and 20 lack a growth boundary, so boundary censoring accompanies the central boundary-ordering failure.

The OAT maps do not identify a dominant gap-control parameter: most upper boundaries are censored, and the only finite pore-D50 and Zener gap responses have two points each. No OAT case passes all pathway gates. Initial pore D50 is nevertheless the strongest measured control of 50 °C/min density attainment. Smaller-D90 and fast smaller-grain signs remain state-dependent.

Successes first appear at a 1.0 µm grain threshold but form no finite window. Eight finite windows appear only after relaxing both grain size to 2.0 µm and density to 0.95; these are diagnostic and not TSS-like. The next step is pore/closed-state and exact-schedule measurement, not another broad search. The model is not validated.
