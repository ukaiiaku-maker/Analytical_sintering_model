# Overlap Mechanism Synthesis

All six fast-firing material hits also admit at least one complete reduced Chen window when only topology/migration parameters are varied. The material dataclass is unchanged between stages, and topology has no path into `rho_dot`. This establishes compatibility of the two hypotheses within the prototype—not validation of a common material.

The common ingredients are distinct:

- fast firing: strongly temperature-sensitive nucleation plus conservative surface/PR redistribution during low-activity exposure;
- two step: connected-pore/TJ drag, persistent junction population, and multihit completion suppress migration without suppressing densification.

No conflict cases appeared among the six selected materials because topology was intentionally excluded from fast-firing densification and the topology search was performed after material selection. That architectural independence is useful, but it also means coexistence is easier than in a fully coupled spatial model.

Discriminating measurements are matched-density Gmean/G50/G90; interrupted-ramp pore D50/D90, tail and connected fraction; nucleation/activity and surface-area-loss proxies; junction density and segment length after T1; and restart grain-growth rates at matched density and grain size. These measurements can determine whether the separated sources are real or merely compensating reduced closures.

Next falsification steps are zero-PR and non-nucleation-limited ablations, full dynamic reintegration of selected Chen windows, and uncertainty bounds that reject extreme grain-ratio cases rather than rewarding them.
