# Six decoder-corrected Tier-B candidates

The production-eligible set contains candidates 693168, 822940, 581668, 295003, 366094, and 85161. All are conditional Tier B prototypes and need calibration.

Candidate 693168 has the largest reported trajectory separation among the final six and a broad 260–275 °C timestep-dependent production window. Its large high-temperature versus nanoscale two-step separation is experimentally relevant rather than a rejection criterion. Its switch state is nevertheless 64.9% closed and therefore calibration-sensitive. Candidate 85161 has an even larger closed fraction (94.2%) but a narrower 60 °C window. Candidates 822940 and 366094 begin with much lower closed fractions (2.2% and 14.7%) and provide useful less-closed comparators.

The family comparison does not establish a universal closed-fraction optimum. Closed fraction alone does not rank window width or reduction monotonically. The common causal evidence is stronger: all six production candidates lose promotion when closed transition, closed shrinkage, or finite closed accommodation is removed; five of six also lose when PR damage is removed.

Candidate 693168 is labeled **calibration-sensitive Tier B with a plausible large separation**. The remaining candidates are labeled plausible or calibration-sensitive Tier B according to preparation growth, closed-store saturation, robustness coverage, and causal evidence—not reduction magnitude alone. Exact values and ablation-loss lists are in the reinterpretation tables.
