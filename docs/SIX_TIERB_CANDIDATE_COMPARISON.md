# Six decoder-corrected Tier-B candidates

The production-eligible set contains candidates 693168, 822940, 581668, 295003, 366094, and 85161. All are conditional Tier B prototypes and need calibration.

Candidate 693168 has the largest reported trajectory separation among the final six and a broad 275 °C production window, but it is also extreme: its switch state is 64.9% closed and its absolute high-temperature grain growth is very large. Candidate 85161 has an even larger closed fraction (94.2%) but a narrower 60 °C window. Candidates 822940 and 366094 begin with much lower closed fractions (2.2% and 14.7%) and provide useful less-closed comparators, although their reductions or preparation growth are weaker.

The family comparison does not establish a universal closed-fraction optimum. Closed fraction alone does not rank window width or reduction monotonically. The common causal evidence is stronger: all six production candidates lose promotion when closed transition, closed shrinkage, or finite closed accommodation is removed; five of six also lose when PR damage is removed.

Candidate 693168 is labeled **extreme but informative**. The remaining candidates are labeled prototype Tier B or calibration-needed according to their preparation growth, closed fraction, and reduction magnitude. Exact values and ablation-loss lists are in `six_tierB_candidate_comparison.csv` and the final-table copy.
