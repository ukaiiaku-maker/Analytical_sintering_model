# Two-Step History-Effect Analysis

Exact first-step paths show matched-density differences in grain size, pore D50/D90, connected-fine proxy, PR exposure, XJ, and Lambda/K. The projection audit indicates that sufficiently persistent differences could modify migration during the second step. It does not demonstrate that any proposed family generates or retains that state through a coupled evolution law.

The bottleneck is therefore model-state specificity, not a lack of candidate parameters. Residual stress, location-resolved pore fractions, connected TJ/GB coverage, and segment length are not present in the separated prototype. Without those observables, family rankings would be aliases of the same reduced state.
