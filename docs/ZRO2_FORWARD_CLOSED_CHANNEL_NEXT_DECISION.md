# Next closed-channel decision

This recommendation is not validation. The main gap is the physical mapping of closed inventory, shrinkable fraction, dimensional prefactor, availability, pore size, and accommodation coupling—not a known material activation barrier outside a universal bound.

The next implementation should derive or explicitly fit a closed-pore shrinkage/accommodation law. Preferred work is: acquire or specify closed-pore radius/connectivity and gas/accommodation evidence; derive a GB-diffusion or renewal-limited dimensional law; then test it on the fixed paths before any broad search. If data are unavailable, an empirical branch may fit a rate scale, but it must remain clearly separated from physical-property claims.

Candidate 693168 is a conditional comparator, not validation or a calibrated law. Its closed fraction at switch (~0.649), accommodation (~0.152), and substantial closed density contribution illustrate the state/rate effect to explain, not values to copy directly.
