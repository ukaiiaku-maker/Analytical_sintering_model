# Nucleation-Fast/Chen Overlap Synthesis

At bounded-map resolution, both frozen materials coexist with at least one migration-only topology closure. E0021 is the preferred common material because its fast-firing mechanism has the cleaner causal timing signature. E0021 windows include q0 closures and a q1 pore-pinned closure; across both materials, q0 and q1 families remain viable.

Topology never enters `rho_dot`; shared-state tests verify exact invariance. The Chen lower boundary is controlled by frozen material densification kinetics, whereas the upper boundary moves with persistent-junction strength, pore drag, and the temperature-dependent Lambda/K completion factor.

This is not full production validation. The strongest windows occur mostly at G0=600 nm and permissive 20% preparation growth. Before claiming a common physical model, the full grid must demonstrate nanoscale windows under 5--10% preparation tolerances and multiple first-step heating rates.

Strict reclassification yields no Tier A candidate. E0142 has five unique Tier B windows, spanning 30--40 C at G1 about 303--304 nm; q0 and q1 both survive. E0021 has Tier C only. Accordingly E0142 is the only strong common candidate for production-style figures, while E0021 remains the cleaner mechanistic fast-firing candidate.
