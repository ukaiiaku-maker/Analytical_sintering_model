# Experimental Constraints for the Next Model

## Priority measurements

1. Matched-density `G_mean`, `G50`, and `G90` versus heating rate, with
   uncertainty sufficient to distinguish ratios 1.2 and 1.5.
2. Pore D50/D90 and large-pore-tail volume versus density.
3. Connected fine-pore fraction and 3D percolation versus density.
4. Pore location partition among GB segments, TJs, isolated, and closed states.
5. Residual stress from diffraction plus local strain mapping near pore/TJ
   clusters.
6. Interrupted slow/fast ramps before density 0.90.
7. High-density 0.95–0.99 pore closure, gas content, and grain growth.
8. Three-dimensional tomography or serial-section connectivity.
9. Two-step restart densification and growth rates at matched density and grain
   size.
10. TJ occupancy, junction mobility, back-stress, and disconnection proxies.

## Direct falsification tests

- **Early PR memory:** falsified if interrupted slow/fast paths show no
  reproducible difference in connected fine pores, pore D90, or surface-area
  loss before density 0.90.
- **Persistent defect memory:** falsified if pore/stress differences disappear
  at matched density without a corresponding grain-tail or local-growth
  response, or if defect-rich regions densify identically to the matrix.
- **Closed-pore late-stage mechanism:** falsified if high-density shrinkage does
  not correlate with closed-pore radius, trapped gas, detachment, or vacancy
  transport, or if no path-dependent signal appears from 0.92 to 0.99.
- **Structural TJ multihit / pore drag:** falsified if matched-state restart
  experiments show no junction-mobility threshold or if pore-filled and clean
  structural TJs have indistinguishable mobility/back-stress response.

## Model-selection measurement

The single most decisive package is synchronized matched-density grain
distributions and 3D pore connectivity at several points along slow and fast
ramps, including at least one point below 0.90 and one above 0.95. It determines
whether the missing state is early connected topology, late closed pores, or
interacting spatial heterogeneity.
