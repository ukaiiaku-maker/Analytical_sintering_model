# Distributional Chen topology

A strict finite Chen window requires a cloned first-step state, a lower density-exhaustion boundary, more than one central success point, an upper grain-growth boundary, nonzero width, and `T2<T1`.

All T2 paths in this test cloned byte-equivalent population states. Thirty-six synthetic successes appeared, but all began at the minimum sampled T2. Consequently:

- lower boundaries: zero successful groups;
- finite windows: zero;
- broad process map: not justified and not run;
- classification: `diagnostic_negative_result`.

The result reproduces the earlier preservation tradeoff in distributional form. Providing enough useful closed inventory for density attainment also creates low-temperature success, erasing the lower boundary. States with less useful inventory remain density-limited or become mixed failures before a success band forms. At high temperature intrinsic grain growth produces the upper failure branch.

No success-colored Chen map is presented. The figure directory contains a failure-mode map and the complete synthetic boundary source table. No validation claim is made.
