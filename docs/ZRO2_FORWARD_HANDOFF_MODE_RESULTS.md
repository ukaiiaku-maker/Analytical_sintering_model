# Open/closed handoff mode results

These are diagnostic forward-model calculations, not validation. `resolved_default` reproduced the inherited result. `diagnostic_open_recovery` substantially restored the fast density to 0.9718 without destroying the smaller-grain sign, but its best strict boundary gap was 0 °C and it produced no success band. Closed-rate factors through 100x and inventory factors through 30x did not materially improve fast density.

The 100x closed-rate case produced a positive boundary-ordering diagnostic of 50 °C in a few groups but zero strict-success points. It is therefore not a finite Chen window. The balanced handoff improved neither fast density nor the strict topology because instantaneous closed availability was too small to justify suppressing the open channel or amplify the closed contribution strongly.

All pathway-gate rows fail the complete gate. Mobility was not used as a rescue.
