# Closed-channel physical mapping

This analysis is not validation. The exact implemented resolved channel factors as closed inventory × accommodation × renewal activity × the PR-derived thermal factor × radius factor × `1/tau0`. Inventory, connectivity, accommodation, activity, pore size, and rate scale are therefore inseparable from the apparent slope.

No physical closed-pore shrinkage/accommodation law is presently defined. A physically mapped implementation would require a selected transport path, local stress or capillary/gas pressure, shrinkable closed inventory, dimensional prefactor, pore-radius exponent, and accommodation evolution. A simple `Q_closed` optimization would instead be an empirical reduced closure, not a physical ZrO2 property model.
