# GB mobility envelope audit

The ZrO2 nucleation barrier and both diffusivity laws were held fixed. Intrinsic GB mobility alone was varied through fixed/current calibration modes, M0 factors from 0.03× to 30×, and activation energies from 300 to 550 kJ/mol with M0 refit at 1500 °C.

No mobility case creates a strict success or finite Chen window once resolved closure-controlled density is enforced. The best mapped boundary gap is −100 °C, compared with −200 °C for the resolved default and the previous 125 °C required-shift benchmark. None passes all pathway gates, and every mode lies outside the conditioned CS endpoint tolerance under the resolved implementation.

The fast smaller-grain sign generally survives, but density and CS trajectory errors remain. Mobility flexibility therefore cannot rescue the resolved pathway within this envelope. No Dong–Chen transition was used, and no validation claim is made.
