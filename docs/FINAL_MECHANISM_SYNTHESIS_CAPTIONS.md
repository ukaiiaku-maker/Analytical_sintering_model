# Final mechanism synthesis caption drafts

These captions describe a conditional mechanism attribution, not validation or paper readiness.

## Figure 1 — Mechanism chains

Fast firing is attributed to nucleation-limited onset behavior: slow heating spends longer in a low-activity growth interval, whereas fast heating crosses that interval quickly. Two-step sintering is attributed to PR-prepared closed-pore accommodation memory: low T2 exhausts densification, intermediate T2 succeeds, and high T2 activates grain growth. Both require serial source/sink renewal, exchange/transport completion, matched-density attainment, and bounded stores. The same material perturbation vector was evaluated in separate model layers.

## Figure 2 — Exact relative-property phase map

Exact-promoted perturbations classified as fast-only, two-step-only, both-pass, or neither in nucleation-dominance and closed-shrinkage/growth coordinates. The 73 both-pass cases establish a finite conditional overlap rather than a unique parameter point. Surrogate rows are excluded and the observed ranges are not universal bounds.

## Figure 3 — Fast-firing property window

The representative exact base trajectory exceeds `G_ref/G_fast = 1.5` over an attained interval. The local `Delta Q_nuc` window is finite: 0 to +50 kJ/mol survives, while -25 and +75 kJ/mol fail. Nucleation-facile ablation removes or weakens most exact passes; PR-off can preserve the effect. Fast firing is therefore attributed to nucleation-limited onset behavior, not PR as the primary cause.

## Figure 4 — Two-step property window

Candidate 693168 produces a large attained high-temperature/two-step grain-size separation, which is the intended experimental-scale signature. The local `Delta Q_closed` survival range is -25 to +100 kJ/mol, with loss of the lower boundary at -50 kJ/mol. The PR prefactor must reach at least 0.3x base in the tested OAT. Candidate 693168 is conditional Tier B, not validation.

## Figure 5 — Chen-window boundaries

The fine exact T2 map for candidate 693168 contains three regimes. The lower boundary is closed-accommodation density exhaustion, the central band attains density with bounded growth, and the upper boundary is grain-growth activation. Closed-pore fraction and accommodation trajectory are the primary calibration targets.

## Figure 6 — Six-candidate Tier-B family

All six exact Tier-B base candidates are compared by reduction, window width, preparation growth, closed fraction at switch, and destructive ablations. Candidate 693168 is the strongest conditional separation comparator, while the others demonstrate distinct Tier-B topologies. Material robustness outside 693168 remains a reduced transfer rather than an exact six-candidate material sweep.

## Figure 7 — Surrogate-versus-exact warning

The dimensionless screen predicts 19,880 both-pass rows, whereas only 73 cases in the exact promoted union pass both separate model layers. Surrogate screens overpredict exact overlap and must not be treated as evidence. They are promotion tools only.

## Figure 8 — Experimental falsification targets

Measurements that can falsify the proposed attribution include matched-density grain distributions, ramp-onset kinetics, 3D pore state, connectivity, trapped-gas accommodation, and grain-growth mobility across T2. The most direct unresolved measurements are closed-pore fraction and accommodation trajectory during interrupted first and second steps.
