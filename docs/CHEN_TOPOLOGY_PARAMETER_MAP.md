# Chen Topology Parameter Map

Material parameters were frozen at the six fast-firing hits. Twenty-four migration-only topology closures retained explicit qTJ=0 and qTJ=1 variants. The reduced map covered G0=75--600 nm, T1=1250--1500 C, switch density 0.75--0.90, and T2 on a 25 C grid below T1.

Of 30,240 topology/first-step combinations, 3,645 had a practical success interval bracketed below by densification exhaustion and above by grain growth. Complete cases included 1,867 qTJ=0 and 1,778 qTJ=1 rows. The response is therefore non-universal and not an artifact of a single q exponent.

Topology changes only the grain-migration factor. Lower bounds come from the frozen material densification rate/capacity; upper bounds come from temperature-activated migration after TJ/pore drag and multihit completion. The diagnostics report XJ, Lambda/K, pore drag, density rate, and predicted growth fraction.

This is a screening map, not a full Chen simulation: the first-step state is conservatively reconstructed at the switch density and the second step is classified from fixed-state 20-hour rate/capacity estimates. Promising windows must be rerun with evolving topology, exact first-step histories, 5% and 10% tolerances, and preparation-growth filters before physical claims.
