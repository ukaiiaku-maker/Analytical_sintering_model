The resulting 440.7–451.8 kJ/mol effective range is near the fast-firing relative envelope: its difference from growth is 35–47 kJ/mol and its difference from 380 kJ/mol transport is 61–72 kJ/mol.

However, effective local `k_nuc/k_transport` is frequently below the promoted prefactor-ratio range, so barrier compatibility does not imply rate compatibility. This rate ratio is a state-dependent proxy, not a newly fitted scalar prefactor.
