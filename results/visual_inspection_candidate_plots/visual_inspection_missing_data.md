# Missing visual-inspection inputs

The separated prototype exposes `X_J`, `Lambda/K`, pore drag, and migration factor, but not `P_comp_TJ`, `C_TJ`, `C_GBseg`, clean-GB fraction, or decomposed power/stress channels. Those quantities were not reconstructed or inferred.

Consequently:

- the 12-panel E0142 triplet is represented by a cross-reference sheet plus the genuine six-panel compact triplet;
- the Chen power/stress figure is a missing-data sheet;
- the E0021 triplet history was not rerun because E0021 has no Tier A/B window; its diagnostic uses the strict tier table;
- dashboards are cross-reference summaries rather than embedded copies of every detailed panel.

Large-pore fraction is also unavailable in the selected fast-history schema. Pore D50, D90, connected-fine fraction, PR exposure, and activity are plotted directly.
